# SaaS / Product playbook

Raw footage is not required unless provided. Priorities:

1. **UI animation** – rebuild key screens as HTML from user screenshots, or place screenshots in
   device/browser frames; animate cursor, clicks, typing, scroll and highlights.
2. **Motion graphics** – shapes, gradients and icons in brand colours only.
3. **Typography** – kinetic headlines (3–6 words per beat), the brand font, max two weights.
4. **Product flow** – show the real flow: problem → open product → do the core action →
   result. Never show features the product doesn't have.
5. **Transitions** – match-cuts on UI elements, zoom-throughs, wipes in brand colour; keep to
   2–3 transition styles per video.
6. **Sound design & music** – clicks, swooshes, UI ticks synced to animations; upbeat bed.

Structure template: Hook/pain (0–3 s) → Product reveal → 2–3 feature beats → Outcome → Logo + CTA.
Tip: `npx hyperframes capture <url>` can capture the user's own website for reference/assets.
